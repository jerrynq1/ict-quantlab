Hybrid architecture

SEQUENCEGRU / LSTM / TransformerResearch candidates, not enabled by default.
FORECAST CONTRACT

What is published?

  • ✓Mutually exclusive scenario probabilities sum to 100%.
  • ✓Price ranges use empirical quantiles of historical analog returns.
  • ✓Event forecast separates buy-side and sell-side sweep.
  • ✓Insufficient sample means no probability is published.
  • ✓Historical replay trains only on data before its as-of timestamp.
